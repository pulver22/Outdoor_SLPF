# ICRA claim-to-evidence matrix

| ID | Manuscript location | Supporting evidence | Permitted wording | Prohibited extension | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C6b | Component ablation | Generated ablation table and six-run-per-variant protocol | Describe measured changes for the registered variants, with exact seeds and traversals. | Calling every component essential, claiming statistical significance, or claiming causal isolation | The ablation is single-site, descriptive, and does not isolate detector accuracy. |
| C6c | Robustness and runtime | Generated robustness/stress tables; CUDA protocol records | Describe behaviour under the registered synthetic profiles and the recorded CUDA host execution. | Claiming arbitrary GNSS-denial robustness, embedded real-time performance, or Jetson throughput | No embedded timing trace or universal-failure evaluation is included. |
| C1 | Introduction; method | Semantic-wall map and generated main table | Finite semantic-wall segments provide row-aligned constraints for the evaluated map. | Claims of universal aliasing resolution or infinite wall geometry | One surveyed vineyard map, finite segment endpoints, and fixed landmark classes. |
| C2 | Experiments | Raw and aligned APE plus RPE in `results/icra_submission/evidence/` | Report map-frame and aligned localisation errors together. | Treating aligned APE as proof of map-frame accuracy | Alignment removes global transform differences. |
| C3 | Operational results | In-row row correctness, cross-track, and wrong-row duration | State row-level behaviour on in-row frames. | Using nearest-row headland labels as unique identity | Headland identity is ambiguous. |
| C4 | GNSS stress | GNSS degradation metrics and stress table | Describe behaviour under the evaluated simulated GNSS degradation profiles. | Claiming GNSS-independent localisation or real outage robustness | Map quality, landmark visibility, and GNSS remain assumptions; stress inputs are controlled. |
| C5 | Baselines | RTAB RGB/RGBD, AMCL, and NoisyGNSS artifacts plus canonical provenance fields | Compare against the recorded, start-pose-anchored baselines and disclose dedicated versus historical references. | Claiming superiority over unmeasured systems or a uniformly fresh benchmark | Baseline preprocessing and provenance are protocol-specific. |
| C6 | Detector description | Detector outputs and model configuration | Describe the detector as an input to localisation. | Claiming detector accuracy without SemanticBLT validation YAML | No supplied validation YAML and no cross-season detector validation. |
| C6a | Detector provenance | `results/icra_submission/evidence/semanticblt_roboflow_metadata.json`; Roboflow SemanticBLT model and dataset pages | Report Roboflow model/dataset-level aggregate benchmark metrics for SemanticBLT v1: precision 76.8%, recall 71.4%, and mAP@50 74.1%. | Treating Roboflow aggregate metrics as independent current-field validation, per-class pole/trunk validation, current-traversal detector recall, or a confusion matrix | External Roboflow benchmark only; no per-class pole/trunk metrics, confusion matrix, or labelled current-traversal validation is available. |
| C7 | Limitations | Dataset/map provenance and protocol JSON | Bound conclusions to the single surveyed vineyard, two traversals, and fixed map. | `generalises across vineyards` or `manual surveying is unnecessary` | No cross-season, cross-vineyard, or real UAV-map validation. |
| C8 | Conclusion | Canonical manifest and claim checks | Claim evaluated row-level localisation and corridor adherence. | `deployment ready`, guaranteed recovery, centimetre-grade, or solving perceptual aliasing | This is an evidence-first submission, not a deployment guarantee. |
