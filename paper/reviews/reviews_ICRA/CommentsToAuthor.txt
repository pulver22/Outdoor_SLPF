Comments to author (Associate Editor)
=====================================

The reviewers have mixed comments about the work. While
they acknowledge that the manuscript is easy to follow,
they also highlighted different sources of concerns about
its current version. Most importantly:

(1) Experimental evaluation (R1, R2) - Both reviewers
raised concerns about the interpretation of the results. R1
mentioned that the plots are not clear and look noisier
than other baseline methods, which raises doubts about the
performance of the system; R2 on the other hand raised
concerns about the comparison being unfair, since the
proposed method uses GPS while the other baselines do not
(e.g., AMCL). In my opinion, after reading these comments
and inspecting the plots, I agree that the results are
difficult to interpret and the advantages of the method do
not seem clear. As suggested by R2, one might wonder why an
AMCL solution with GPS measurements wouldnt outperform all
the other methods. Furthermore, reporting RPE without
specifying the distance traveled (RPE@X meters) is not
informative, because it cannot be interpreted as a drift
rate.

(2) Advantages of the semantics (R1, R2) - Both reviewers
raised concerns to understand how the semantic walls
contribute, both methodologically and quantitatively. This
is a crucial point since this is the main contribution
claimed by the paper, so  it needs to be clear in the
method section, and demonstrated empirically through
experiments that highlight the advantages of this feature.

(3) Clarity of the plots (R2) - Both the labels and the
lines are difficult to see in some plots, especially in Fig
1 and 4. This impedes readers from understanding the
results.

I recommend the authors to carefully review the comments
provided by the reviewers, in order to improve the clarity
of the manuscript and ensure that the claimed technical
contributions are clearly demonstrated by the experiments.
