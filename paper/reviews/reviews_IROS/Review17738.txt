Reviewer 13 of IROS 2026 submission 1263

Comments to the author
======================

This paper presents a robot localization method in
vineyards utilizing the semantic landmark particle filter.
The concept of developing a new robot localizer considering
the agricultural environmental challenges is interesting,
and the authors handle an important problem in precision
agriculture and agricultural automation.

The paper is well organized, and the authors have conducted
various significant ablation studies to show the novelty of
the proposed approach. However, the reviewer has a concern
about the performance of the proposed method in this paper.


The concept that the authors have proposed, which
incorporates the panoptic segmentations results with GNSS
data, is reasonable. However, the demonstrated quantitative
results do not look promising. Even if the proposed method
estimated the robot poses more accurate than other baseline
methods, such as AMCL+NoisyGNSS, RTAB-Map RGBD, and
NoisyGNSS, the proposed method still lacks the accuracy as
the estimated robot pose trajectory by SLPF still crosses
the row lines. It is unrealistic and difficult to be used
in the real agricultural applications. Of course, in the
perspective of research, it is a great progress for
improving the robot localization in vineyard. However, it
is still difficult to say that the proposed method
outperforms other baselines.

Also, quantitative results in the tables show that the RMSE
is close to 1 meter. This error is relatively large
regarding the robot localization. 

In conclusion, the paper is well organized, but the
reviewer suggests the authors to conduct additional
research to improve its performance.
